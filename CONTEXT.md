# Trip planning vocabulary

- **Saved place / Địa điểm đã lưu**: A destination or venue with its own identity and optional address, coordinates, website, or Google Maps place link. Activities may point to it.
- **Route link / Tuyến đường**: A link showing directions between two or more points, such as a Google Maps directions URL. It is not a saved place. An activity may cite it for travel instructions.
- **Reference / Nguồn tham khảo**: An external information page supporting an activity, such as a venue's official hours or onsen details. It is not a saved place or a route.
- **Source link / Liên kết**: The shared storage and citation mechanism for route links and references. Its category determines the label shown to travelers.
